# Furia Leather — editorial photography

Use the same female and male model throughout the homepage. Export JPG or WebP files at 85–90% quality, without text, logos, borders, or filters.

## Female model (`women/`)

- `hero-women.jpg` — portrait 4:5, minimum 1600 × 2000 px; waist-up, looking toward camera.
- `women-full.jpg` — portrait 4:5, minimum 1600 × 2000 px; full outfit and jacket visible.
- `women-wide.jpg` — landscape 3:2, minimum 2200 × 1467 px; natural outdoor/editorial scene.
- `women-detail.jpg` — landscape 3:2, minimum 1800 × 1200 px; leather texture, zip, collar, or hands.

## Male model (`men/`)

- `hero-men.jpg` — portrait 4:5, minimum 1600 × 2000 px; waist-up, looking toward camera.
- `men-full.jpg` — portrait 4:5, minimum 1600 × 2000 px; full outfit and jacket visible.
- `men-wide.jpg` — landscape 3:2, minimum 2200 × 1467 px; natural outdoor/editorial scene.
- `men-detail.jpg` — landscape 3:2, minimum 1800 × 1200 px; leather texture, zip, collar, or hands.

Keep skin texture natural, use one consistent light and color treatment, and avoid synthetic-looking backgrounds or excessive retouching.
